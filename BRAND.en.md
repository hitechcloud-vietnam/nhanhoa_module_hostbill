## Module naming

Keep the technical names, lowercase spelling, and underscores when referring to paths or code: `nhanhoa_cloud`, `nhanhoa_dns`, `nhanhoa_domain`, `nhanhoa_email`, `nhanhoa_hosting`, and `nhanhoa_ssl`.

Use **Nhan Hoa** in English prose and preserve **Nhân Hòa** in Vietnamese prose. Retain the original spelling in API names, package names, URLs, and technical labels. Spell **HostBill** consistently.
